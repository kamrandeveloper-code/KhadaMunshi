# KhadMunshi Launch Kit: Video Script, Captions, Blog Plan

Oct 26, 2026 · @M Akmal

## 1. Name and domain

**KhadMunshi** is a good product name: every Pakistani and Indian shopkeeper knows both words (khata = ledger, munshi = the trusted clerk who keeps it), so it explains the product before you do. Write it as one word with two capitals everywhere: KhadMunshi.

- **Tagline:** *KhadMunshi: aapki dukaan ka digital munshi.*
- **Address:** `KhadMunshi.yourdomain.com` for the landing page and demo request. Put the blog at `KhadMunshi.yourdomain.com/blog`, not on a second subdomain. Google treats `blog.KhadMunshi...` as a separate site, so a blog on the same address passes its search strength to the product page.
- **Before you print the name anywhere:** search "KhadMunshi" on Google and the Play Store, and check trademark search in Pakistan (IPO) and India. Khata apps are a crowded space and you do not want to rename after 10,000 views.
- **Later:** a standalone `KhadMunshi.com` or `.pk` looks more premium than a subdomain of a developer domain. Buy it when the first 5 shops are paying, and redirect the subdomain to it.
- **Video end card:** replace `kamrandeveloper.com` with the KhadMunshi address and your own WhatsApp number, so the name on screen is the name you are building.

### How to film it

- **Two phones.** Phone A only records you. Phone B only runs the app and screen-records it. Do not film Phone B's screen with Phone A except for the 1 second where it fills the frame.
- **Make the cut invisible.** Before you lift Phone B, open the dashboard on it, and start the screen recording on the same dashboard. In the editor, cut from the camera shot to the screen recording the instant the screen fills the frame. A quick zoom and whoosh sound at that frame hides the join.
- **Hold the register open** so the crossed-out, messy page is visible. That page is your problem shot, so use the worst real page you have.
- **Demo data only.** Use made-up farmer names and amounts on screen. Never show a real customer's balance in a public video.
- **Phone B settings.** Turn on Do Not Disturb, set brightness to maximum, and on Android turn on Show Taps (Developer options) so viewers see where you press.
- **Laptop shot.** Use a screen recorder (OBS, or Win+G on Windows) at 1920×1080. Do not film the laptop with a phone.
- **Phone A settings.** Vertical, 1080×1920, 30 fps, tripod or phone propped on books at eye level, light from a window in front of you, fertilizer bags visible behind you if you can film in the shop.
- **Audio.** Record in a quiet room. Speak at normal speed and leave about half a second between sentences so you can cut tightly.
- **Editing.** CapCut's auto-captions will do Roman Urdu or Urdu. Put captions in the lower middle, above the app's own buttons. Cut every 2 to 3 seconds. Add soft background music at low volume.
- **Export and transfer.** Export at 1080×1920. Move the file with a cable, Google Drive or Quick Share. Do not send it through WhatsApp, which crushes quality to the 478×850 your last video had.
- **Only show what the app really has.** If a screen above (supplier purchase, stock list) looks different in your app, show your real screen and adjust the line.

### Posting rules that protect your account

- Read each group's rules first. If promotion is banned, message the admin and ask permission; a yes from the admin is worth more than ten ignored posts.
- Post in 3 to 5 groups a day, not 50 at once. Mass posting looks like spam to both admins and Facebook.
- Reply to every comment within an hour, in Roman Urdu. Early replies make the post show up for more people.
- Do not write "comment DEMO to get a link". Facebook treats that as engagement bait. Ask a real question, as in Version A.
- If a group blocks phone numbers in posts, put the WhatsApp number in the first comment instead.
- Use the same video in every group, but change the version of the caption.

## 5. Blog plan (English, for Pakistani and Indian dealers)

**My preference:** write posts 1, 3 and 4 first. Rabi (wheat) sowing and the DAP and urea rush are happening now, so post 1 matches what shopkeepers are worrying about this month; post 3 is a problem almost no software talks about; post 4 is what a buyer searches just before choosing software. Every post should be 1,200 to 1,800 words, include one free downloadable sheet (for example an udhaar tracker), and end with one line inviting a demo. Use Roman Urdu terms (khata, udhaar, munshi) in subheadings, because that is how people search.

Rules in posts 5 and 6 change. Check each point against the current official text before you publish.

## 6. Posting checklist and what to measure

Judge the new video after 48 hours using three numbers from each platform's insights, compared with your older 500 to 1,000 view videos.

- [ ] Re-record using the script in section 2, export at 1080×1920, and transfer without WhatsApp
- [ ] Replace the end card with KhadMunshi branding, your WhatsApp number and the new web address
- [ ] Post on the pages and personal profiles with the captions in section 3, at different times of day
- [ ] Post the question-first version in 3 to 5 relevant groups per day, after reading each group's rules
- [ ] Reply to every comment and message within an hour
- [ ] Record a second version with a different first 3 seconds (for example, start with the dashboard instead of the register) so you can test which opening works
- [ ] Publish blog post 1 and share a short Roman Urdu summary in the groups

| What to look at | What it tells you | If it is low |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3-second view rate (or "viewed past the first few seconds") | Whether the opening works | Change the first 3 seconds: bigger text, a sharper register shot, a faster line |
| Average watch time or retention graph | Where people leave | Cut the screen that loses people; keep every shot under 4 seconds |
| WhatsApp messages per 1,000 views | Whether the ending works | Make the number bigger, hold the end card longer, say the number aloud |

Posting times are a test, not a rule: try about 12:30 pm and 8 pm Pakistan time for a week each and keep whichever gets more watch time.
